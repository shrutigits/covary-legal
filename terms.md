# Covary terms of use

You accept these terms on the Consent screen at sign-up, when you tap "Agree and continue". They are short on purpose. How Covary handles your data is in the [privacy policy](https://shrutigits.github.io/covary-legal/).

## 1. Who runs Covary

Covary is run by Shruti Dwivedi, 8 Enggor St, Singapore 079718. For any question about these terms, write to appsforbetterlife330@gmail.com. A person reads this address. The app does not answer it.

## 2. What Covary is

Covary is a wellness app. It is not a medical device. "This app does not provide a diagnosis or medical advice. Only a doctor can do that." Use Covary to understand your body and to prepare for a doctor's visit, not to replace one.

## 3. Your account

- You need an invite code to create an account. A code works once.
- You can have one account for each phone number.
- You must be at least 16, and at least 18 in India and Indonesia. Covary is closed in some countries. The list in the app decides.
- Your age is checked once, at sign-up, against the country and birth date you enter. Covary does not check them again later.
- Your birth date, sex at birth, country, state and city cannot be changed after sign-up. Give true answers.

## 4. The companion

The companion is an AI. It can be wrong. It is built not to diagnose, name conditions, judge how urgent something is, give doses or call a day safe. If your words signal a crisis, the app shows an emergency card. You can send up to 60 companion messages a day and 10 a minute.

## 5. Your health data and your choices

How Covary handles your health data is in the privacy policy. You make four choices at sign-up, and one more (medical research) in Settings. None is ticked for you. "Store and analyse my health data" cannot be switched off while you keep your account: switching it off opens the screen for deleting your account.

## 6. The community

- You agree to the rules shown in the app before you join the community, and again each time the rules change.
- An AI check can hold a post until a person looks at it.
- A post that two members report is hidden until a person decides.
- A person running Covary can also warn, suspend or permanently remove a member after a report about her.
- You can post up to 20 times a day, and up to 5 times a day in your first 72 hours.
- When a post is removed, you are told which rule it broke.
- Three removals in 30 days give a 7-day suspension from posting and replying.
- A second suspension ends your place in the community for good. A permanently removed member cannot open a new Covary account with the same phone number. If the removal is overturned on appeal, the phone number is cleared.
- You can appeal a removal, a suspension or a permanent removal once. The app says you will hear back within 24 hours.
- A suspension or a permanent removal stops you from posting and replying only. You can still read, log, use the companion and sign in.
- Apart from blocking that phone number from signing up again, these actions apply in the community only. Covary does not otherwise close accounts.

## 7. Export and deleting your account

You can download your data and delete your account in Settings. Deleting removes your data, your key, your files and your journal. Your posts are removed too, except posts that other members replied to: those stay as "Post deleted". Backups clear within 35 days. Covary keeps a record that an account was deleted, invite records with a keyed hash of the invited phone number (a scrambled, one-way copy) for up to 5 years, and, for a permanently removed community member, a keyed hash of her phone number for as long as Covary runs, unless the removal is overturned on appeal. Some audit lines also stay. The privacy policy has the full list.

## 8. Changes

When these terms change, the new version is published at the Terms of use link in Settings. A future material change also raises the consent version, which shows the Consent screen again.

## 9. No warranty

Covary is provided "as is" and "as available". Covary does not promise that it will always work, be free of errors or be available. Nothing in Covary is medical advice.

## 10. Limit of liability

The most Covary can owe you in total for any loss from using the app is the amount you paid Covary in the 12 months before your claim. If you paid nothing, that amount is zero. This does not limit anything the law does not allow to be limited.
