# Delete your Covary account

## In the app

Open Settings, then tap "Delete my account". Your key is destroyed, your files and any export you built are deleted, every health row is hard-deleted, comparison results are cleared, and your phone is wiped of its local copy and its tokens. Section 7 of the [privacy policy](./) lists exactly what is deleted.

## Without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email appsforbetterlife330@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account" and the mobile number you signed up with. The account and everything listed in section 7 of the privacy policy is deleted, and you get a reply when it is done. Requests are answered within one month.

## The test version

The version of Covary in TestFlight and Google Play testing is a demo. It creates no account on any server, and everything it holds is on your phone. "Delete my account" in Settings clears it, and uninstalling the app removes everything.
